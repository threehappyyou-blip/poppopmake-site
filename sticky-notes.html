How your data is protected — exactly

On your device only

Everything you write is stored in your browser on your phone or computer. The app has no server and no account. Its page is set up with a security policy that blocks its scripts from connecting to any other website, and it loads no analytics or ad scripts.

Encrypted before saving

Entries, notes, diary pages and photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your passcode (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds) and separately by a recovery code shown to you once.

What it can’t do

We can’t reset your passcode or recover your data. It can’t protect against someone who knows your passcode or malware on your device. A short number passcode is weaker — use a longer one for strong protection.

Questions people ask

Are the sticky notes free?

Yes. No sign-up, subscription or paid tier.

Where are my notes saved?

Only in your browser on your device, encrypted. They save automatically while you type and are never uploaded.

Can I keep a password or private note here?

The notes are encrypted and locked with your passcode, which protects them from someone picking up your phone. For important passwords, a dedicated password manager with its own recovery options is still the safer choice.

Will my notes be on my other devices?

Not automatically. Make a backup file on one device and restore it on the other to copy everything over.

What if I delete a note by mistake?

Tap Undo in the message that appears right after deleting. After that, only a backup file made earlier can bring it back.
