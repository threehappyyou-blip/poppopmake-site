This is a projection, not a promise. Investment returns aren't guaranteed and will vary year to year — 7% is a common long-run planning assumption, not a forecast. Years are 12-month periods starting today, and your raise is applied once a year. Contribution limits use the 2026 IRS figures ($24,500 base, plus age-based catch-up, a $360,000 cap on the pay a plan can count and a $72,000 cap on total yearly additions) and are assumed to rise with your inflation setting in later years, since the IRS adjusts them for inflation. Your employer match is worked out on what you can actually contribute after those limits. It doesn't account for taxes on withdrawal, vesting schedules on employer match, tiered match formulas, or fees. Your numbers never leave your device — see how to check that yourself.
