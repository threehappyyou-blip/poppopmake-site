Using a tool like a phone app

Our most-used calculators can be added to your phone's home screen and opened like a regular app, with their own icon and no browser address bar. Open the tool, then:

  • Android / Chrome: tap the "Install" bar that appears near the top of the page.
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen".

Currently available on: Paycheck Calculator, UK Paycheck Calculator, Canada Paycheck Calculator, Australia Paycheck Calculator, Mortgage Calculator, 401(k) Retirement Calculator, Debt Payoff Calculator, BMI Calculator, Calorie Calculator, and Screenshot Markup. This installs a home-screen shortcut, not an App Store or Play Store app — the tool still runs from the website.
