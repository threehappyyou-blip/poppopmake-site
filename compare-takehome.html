Why just four countries? Tools covering 100+ countries rely on exchange rates that shift daily and cost-of-living indexes that are, at best, informed estimates. We'd rather be exactly right about four systems we've verified in real depth — every US state, both sides of the UK's tax split, Australia, and all 13 Canadian provinces and territories — than approximately right about all of them. If you need a rough global comparison, larger international tools exist for that. If you need the precise number for one of these four, this is built for exactly that.
