How to set it as your wallpaper

iPhone

Open the picture in Photos → Share → Use as Wallpaper. Pinch to fit, then choose Lock Screen.

Android / Galaxy

Open the picture in Gallery → ⋮ → Set as wallpaper → Lock screen (or both).

Desktop

Windows: right-click the file → Set as desktop background. Mac: System Settings → Wallpaper → Add Photo.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Make as many as you like for your own phone or computer. No account, nothing is uploaded — the picture is drawn in your browser.

Can I share it?

Yes, share it with friends or post it with credit to PopPopMake. Please don’t sell the pictures or the illustrations.

Why does it look sharp on my phone?

Each scene is a vector drawing rendered at your exact screen size, like 1179×2556 for iPhone.

Can it show my moods?

In the free PopPopMake diary, Diary → “Wallpaper” draws the same calendar with each day’s mood face.

Which day does the week start on?

Sunday or Monday — your choice. Japanese pages start on Monday by default.
