This is an estimate, not tax advice. It uses 2026 federal brackets (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and the 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax. Your sales are netted the way Schedule D does it (short-term against short-term, long-term against long-term, then the two against each other), up to $3,000 of a net loss ($1,500 if married filing separately) lowers your other income, and the net gain is stacked on top of your other taxable income. MAGI for NIIT is approximated as your other income plus the net gain, which can differ from your real MAGI. It doesn't include state tax, wash-sale rules, the higher rates on collectibles or real-estate depreciation, or Section 1202/QSBS treatment.
