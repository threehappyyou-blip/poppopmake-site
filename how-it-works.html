Transparency

How This Actually Works

Every disclaimer on this site says some version of "runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded." That's a promise. Here's how to stop taking our word for it and check yourself — it takes about a minute.

There's no account system on this site, and no server sitting behind these tools collecting what you type. When you merge a PDF, run a paycheck calculation, or build an invoice, the work happens on your device, using JavaScript that runs in your browser — the same way a spreadsheet app on your computer doesn't need the internet to add two numbers. Your file, your income, your resume text: none of it has anywhere to go, because nothing on the other end is listening.

Check it yourself, right now

  1. Open your browser's Developer Tools — F12 on Windows and Linux, or Cmd Option I on a Mac.
  2. Click the Network tab.
  3. Go use any tool on this site — merge a couple of PDFs, run the paycheck calculator, build an invoice, whatever you'd normally do.
  4. Watch the list while you do it. You'll see this page's own files load once, right at the start. After that, doing your actual task adds nothing new to that list — no request carries your file, your numbers, or your text anywhere.

The one exception

The Feedback form is different, on purpose. When you submit it, what you type is sent to an inbox through a form service called Web3Forms, because the entire point of that page is for a person to read it. Every calculator, converter, and generator on this site is not like that. If a tool doesn't ask you to click "Submit" to send something to a person, it isn't sending anything anywhere.

Why build it this way

Running everything in the browser has a real cost: very large files are slower than they'd be on a beefy server doing the work for you. In exchange, there's no upload step, no account to lose access to, and no copy of your resume or your invoice sitting on a server somewhere that could someday be breached, sold, or subpoenaed. For what these tools do, that trade seemed worth it.

No limits, on purpose

There's no file-size cap, no daily task limit, and no watermark slapped across your download to nudge you toward a paid plan. That's not generosity — it's just honest. A server costs money for every file it processes, so services built that way eventually need a reason to make you pay: a cap here, a watermark there, a "Pro" button that shows up right when you need it most. Since your own device is doing the work here, not ours, there's no per-file cost pushing that behavior — so there's nothing to gate. Process one file or ten thousand; it costs us the same either way.

What we don't do

A short list, kept honest by being short enough to actually check:

  • No forced account or email wall to use a tool
  • No watermark on anything you generate here
  • No fake countdown timers or "3 people are viewing this" pressure
  • No ad between one click and the next — ads sit around the tools, never inside the flow of using one
  • No burying the "no thanks" option smaller or greyer than the "yes" one, anywhere on this site — including the little thank-you card you might see after a tool finishes

If you ever find a place this site does one of those things anyway, that's a bug, not a feature — tell us and we'll fix it. Fixes, catches, and everything shipped are logged on the Changelog. For keyboard and screen-reader support specifically, see the Accessibility Statement.
