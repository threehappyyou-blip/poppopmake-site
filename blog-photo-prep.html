    Always done: location and camera details are removed, and sideways phone photos are turned upright.

    1. Why resize? A phone photo is often 3–6 MB and 4000 px wide. Blogs show it at about 700–1200 px, so most of that weight only slows your page down.
    2. Why remove the location? Phone photos can carry the exact GPS spot where they were taken — often your home. Re-saving here leaves it out.
    3. Why rename? “seoul-cafe-01.jpg” tells search engines and readers what the picture is; “IMG_4821.jpg” tells them nothing.
    How this works: Your browser opens each photo, draws it at the new size and saves it again — that is what drops the location and camera details. Nothing is uploaded; face covering uses a small detector that runs on your device. Check covered faces before you post: automatic detection can miss a face that is small, turned away or partly hidden. Need to fine-tune one photo? Use Face Mosaic or Screenshot Markup.
