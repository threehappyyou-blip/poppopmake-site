How your data is protected — exactly

On your device only

Everything you write is stored in your browser on your phone or computer. The app has no server and no account. Its page is set up with a security policy that blocks its scripts from connecting to any other website, and it loads no analytics or ad scripts.

Encrypted before saving

Entries, notes, diary pages and photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your passcode (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds) and separately by a recovery code shown to you once.

What it can’t do

We can’t reset your passcode or recover your data. It can’t protect against someone who knows your passcode or malware on your device. A short number passcode is weaker — use a longer one for strong protection.

Questions people ask

Is the budget tracker really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, subscription or paid tier. The introduction pages on this site show ads; the app screen itself does not.

Can PopPopMake see my spending?

No. Your entries are encrypted and stored only in your browser on your device. The app has no server, and we never receive your data, passcode or recovery code.

What happens if I forget my passcode?

Use the recovery code you saved during setup to set a new passcode. If you lose both, nobody can open the data, so keep the recovery code and a backup file somewhere safe.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes, but they do not sync automatically. Make a backup file on one device and restore it on the other; restoring replaces what is on that device.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add entries yourself, tap a frequent entry, or import a CSV statement downloaded from your bank. The file is read on your device only.
