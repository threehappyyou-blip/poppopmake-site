This is an estimate, not tax advice. It follows the CRA's 2026 payroll-deduction formulas (T4127): federal and provincial/territorial tax brackets, the basic personal amounts, the credits for CPP/QPP and EI premiums, the Canada employment amount, the deduction for the enhanced part of CPP/QPP, Ontario's surtax and Health Premium, and British Columbia's tax reduction. Quebec uses its own QPP, QPIP and EI rates, Revenu Québec's tax brackets and basic personal amount, Quebec's deduction for workers and the 16.5% federal abatement. It doesn't include RRSP or other deductions, spouse, dependant or other credits, union dues, benefit or pension-plan contributions, or credits you only receive when you file (such as the GST/HST credit, Quebec's solidarity credit, or the Canada Workers Benefit). Any of these can change your real paycheque. Confirm anything important with the CRA (or Revenu Québec) or your payroll department.
