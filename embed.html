Drop a live, working PopPopMake tool into your own site with one script tag. No signup, no API key, no iframe wrangling on your end — it resizes itself. Free to use, forever, on any site.

Paycheck Calculator

All 50 states plus D.C., federal tax, FICA — the same calculator that's on this site, just sized to fit in a sidebar or inside an article.

Live preview
<div data-poppopmake-widget="paycheck-calculator"></div>
<script src="https://poppopmake.com/embed-loader.js" async></script>
How it works: The script finds any <div data-poppopmake-widget> on your page, drops in an iframe pointed at our servers, and listens for a resize message so it never leaves dead space or a scrollbar. It only loads on pages where you've actually placed the snippet — nothing runs site-wide. The one thing we ask in return is the small "Powered by PopPopMake" line at the bottom; it's how people find their way back here.
