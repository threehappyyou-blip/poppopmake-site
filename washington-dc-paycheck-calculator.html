Worked example

A single filer in Washington DC earning $65,000/year, paid biweekly: federal income tax is about $5,620, FICA (Social Security + Medicare) is about $4,973, and Washington DC's state tax comes to about $2,850/year in this example, on top of federal tax and FICA. That leaves roughly $51,557/year take-home (about $1,983 per biweekly paycheck) — an effective tax rate of about 20.7%. Change the salary above to see your own number.

How Washington DC's income tax brackets work

Washington DC taxes income across 7 brackets. Income is taxed in slices, so moving into a higher bracket only raises the rate on the portion of income inside it — not your entire income. For a single filer, the brackets break down like this:

Taxable income (single filer)Rate
$0 – $10,0004%
$10,000 – $40,0006%
$40,000 – $60,0006.5%
$60,000 – $250,0008.5%
$250,000 – $500,0009.25%
$500,000 – $1,000,0009.75%
$1,000,000 and up10.75%

Washington DC applies a standard deduction ($15,000 single / $30,000 married filing jointly — the 2025 amounts, the latest the state has published) before any of these rates apply. Washington DC taxes residents under these seven brackets with no separate city-level income tax layered on top — DC's brackets already function as both the "state" and "local" tax in one, since there's no larger state to sit inside of.

Frequently asked questions

What's the state income tax rate in Washington DC?

Washington DC uses a progressive state income tax that tops out at 10.75% for the highest earners. This calculator applies it the same way the state does — see the breakdown table above for the exact dollar amount at your income.

Why might my real paycheck be different from this estimate?

This tool uses 2026 federal brackets and Washington DC's 2026 state tax rules with the standard deduction, plus whatever you add under More options (401(k), health/HSA, bonus, overtime, a spouse's pay, children). It doesn't know about your actual W-4 elections, itemized deductions, most state credits and dependent exemptions, or local (city or county) income taxes — all of which can shift your real number. Treat it as a solid planning estimate, not your exact paycheck.

Does this calculator send my salary or personal details anywhere?

No. The whole calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript — nothing you type is transmitted, logged, or stored on a server.

Can I compare Washington DC against another state or country?

What's the standard deduction in Washington DC for 2026?

$15,000 for single filers ($30,000 married filing jointly) — the 2025 amounts, the latest the state has published, applied before the state tax brackets above.

Is Washington DC's income tax the same everywhere in the state?

Yes, for state tax. Washington DC taxes residents under these seven brackets with no separate city-level income tax layered on top — DC's brackets already function as both the "state" and "local" tax in one, since there's no larger state to sit inside of.
