Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This explains what PopPopMake ("we," "us") collects when you visit poppopmake.com, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Files you process

Most tools run entirely in your browser. Files you convert, merge, or edit stay on your device and are never uploaded to us, unless a specific tool's page clearly says otherwise.

Usage data

We use analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand how the site is used — which pages are visited, general location at the country/city level, device type, and referring site. This is aggregated and isn't used to identify you personally.

Cookies

We use cookies for essential site function, analytics, and advertising. See "Cookies and advertising" below.

Anything you send us

If you email us, or use the feedback form or the request form on the Help Me Find a Tool page, we keep that correspondence so we can respond and follow up. Both forms are delivered to our inbox through Web3Forms, a third-party form service — submissions aren't stored on this site and aren't visible to other visitors. The request form also passes along a few details with your message: the page it was sent from, your browser's language setting, and which tools the finder suggested. The words you type into the finder itself are matched in your browser and aren't sent anywhere; only a request you choose to send is. If you add an email address, we use it only to reply about that request.

Budget, Notes & Diary app

The Budget, Notes & Diary app (at poppopmake.com/vault) keeps your budget entries, notes, diary entries, diary photos and settings only in your browser's storage on your own device. We do not receive, store or have any way to read them, and the app has no account and no server. Before anything is saved it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key protected by your passcode and, separately, by a recovery code. We never see your passcode or recovery code, so we cannot reset it or recover your data.

  • No analytics or ads in the app. The app page loads no analytics, advertising or other third-party scripts, and is set up with a security policy that blocks its scripts from connecting to any other website.
  • What is kept outside the encryption: only your language and display preferences, a counter of wrong passcode attempts and, if you put notes on your computer’s desktop, which notes those are (by a random id) and where their windows sit on the screen, stored in your browser. None of these contain your entries or notes.
  • Desktop note windows are more windows of the same app on the same computer. The open session is handed only to windows the app opened itself, never over a channel other pages could listen to.
  • Fingerprint / face unlock (optional) uses a passkey kept by your phone’s or computer’s own password manager (for example iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or Windows Hello), which may sync it between your devices under that company’s terms. The app keeps only the passkey’s id, a random value and an encrypted copy of the unlock key; your fingerprint or face never reaches the app or us, and nothing is sent to PopPopMake.
  • Diary decorations (paper, stickers, pages, drawings) are encrypted with the entry; a drawing is saved as its own encrypted picture. The sticker art and handwriting fonts are files on this site, so choosing them contacts no other website.
  • Backup files you download are encrypted and stay wherever you put them. CSV exports are not encrypted — anyone who opens one can read it.
  • Diary photos are made smaller and encrypted on your device before they are saved; they are never uploaded. A diary saved as a text file from Settings is not encrypted.
  • Bank statement import reads the CSV file on your device; the file is not uploaded.
  • Like every page on this site, the app's files are delivered by our hosting provider, which processes standard connection data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver them. That data never includes what you type into the app.
  • To delete everything, use Settings → Erase everything on this device, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Cookies and advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense to keep tools free. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Center or aboutads.info.

Some tools may use rewarded-video ad providers, which can set their own cookies for the duration of an ad.

Children's privacy

PopPopMake isn't directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age where you live), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

We keep analytics data only as long as it's useful for the purposes above, and correspondence you send us until we no longer need it.

Third-party links and services

Some pages may link to or use third-party services (for example, an ad network or a font provider). Their own privacy policies govern your interaction with them.

We built PopPopMake's tools to run in your browser specifically so that most of what you work on never has to touch our servers at all. Where that changes for a specific tool, we'll say so plainly on that tool's page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we add tools or services. We'll update the date at the top when we do.
