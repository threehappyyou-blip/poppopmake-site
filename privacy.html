Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This explains what PopPopMake ("we," "us") collects when you visit poppopmake.com, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Files you process

Most tools run entirely in your browser. Files you convert, merge, or edit stay on your device and are never uploaded to us, unless a specific tool's page clearly says otherwise.

Usage data

We use analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand how the site is used — which pages are visited, general location at the country/city level, device type, and referring site. This is aggregated and isn't used to identify you personally.

Cookies

We use cookies for essential site function, analytics, and advertising. See "Cookies and advertising" below.

Anything you send us

If you email us, or use the feedback form, we keep that correspondence so we can respond and follow up. The feedback form is delivered to our inbox through Web3Forms, a third-party form service — submissions aren't stored on this site and aren't visible to other visitors.

Cookies and advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense to keep tools free. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Center or aboutads.info.

Some tools may use rewarded-video ad providers, which can set their own cookies for the duration of an ad.

Your choices

WhoWhat you can do
EveryoneUse the cookie banner shown on your first visit to accept or decline non-essential (analytics/advertising) cookies.
EU / EEA / UK visitorsUnder GDPR, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and object to processing. Since we collect very little personal data, most requests can be handled directly — just email us.
California visitorsUnder the CCPA/CPRA, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context advertising. Because we use third-party ad cookies, declining them in our cookie banner is the most direct way to opt out.

Children's privacy

PopPopMake isn't directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age where you live), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention

We keep analytics data only as long as it's useful for the purposes above, and correspondence you send us until we no longer need it.

Third-party links and services

Some pages may link to or use third-party services (for example, an ad network or a font provider). Their own privacy policies govern your interaction with them.

We built PopPopMake's tools to run in your browser specifically so that most of what you work on never has to touch our servers at all. Where that changes for a specific tool, we'll say so plainly on that tool's page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we add tools or services. We'll update the date at the top when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise any of the choices above? Email hello@poppopmake.com.
