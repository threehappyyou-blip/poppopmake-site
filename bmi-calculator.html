BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis. It doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, and can misclassify very muscular or very lean people, older adults, and pregnant women. Treat it as one data point — for anything about your own health, a doctor who can look at the whole picture is the right call, not a formula.
