What's Changed

"Built in the open" is easy to put in a tagline. This page is what that actually means: what shipped, what broke and got caught before anyone saw it, and — once they start coming in — what got fixed because someone used the Feedback form.

Three kinds of entries show up here: Shipped for something new, Caught in testing for a bug found and fixed before launch, and Reported for a fix that started with someone else noticing first. That last tag is empty today — it won't stay that way.

A dedicated paycheck calculator page for every U.S. state

50 states plus D.C., each with its own page, its own URL, and an opening line pulled directly from the same verified tax data the calculator itself uses — no state income tax, a flat rate, or a progressive top rate, whichever is actually true. Aug 27, 2026
Caught in testing

Two of the three new Everyday Tools would have shipped completely broken

A leftover line from development (a Node.js-only statement that has no meaning in a browser) got left in the Age Calculator's script block. It would have thrown the moment the page loaded, and because JavaScript stops running the rest of a script block after an uncaught error, everything after it — including the calculator's own setup — would never have run. Separately, the Percentage and Tip calculators shared a helper function that was actually defined in a different, disconnected block of code, so calling it would have thrown too, silently breaking both of those tools' button and input wiring before it ever reached the working math underneath. All three only surfaced by actually simulating clicks and typing in a real, in-memory browser rather than just checking the calculations in isolation. Both fixed, and every interaction re-tested afterward.

Shipped

Capital Gains Tax Calculator

Stocks and crypto together, since the IRS taxes both identically. Handles the part most people get wrong: a gain doesn't get its own tax bracket — it stacks on top of your other income, so the same $70,000 gain can be tax-free for one household and taxed at 23.8% for another depending on what else they earned. Includes the 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax and correctly splits a large gain across multiple brackets when it spans more than one. Tested against a five-scenario suite, including a gain deliberately sized to cross all three long-term brackets at once, before shipping.

Aug 25, 2026
Caught in testing

Ontario surtax was compounding incorrectly

The two Ontario surtax tiers (20% and 36%) were being applied one on top of the other instead of both independently against the original provincial tax — a subtle bug that only showed up when checked against a known reference rate (20.53% combined marginal at the top bracket; the buggy version gave 21.48%). Fixed before the Canada calculator shipped.

Shipped

Accessibility pass: contrast, keyboard, screen readers

Every text/background color combination on the site had its contrast ratio calculated, not eyeballed — the link color and primary button color were both failing WCAG AA (4.12:1 and 3.45:1, needed 4.5:1) and got darkened just enough to pass. Added skip-to-content links, fixed two skipped heading levels, added labels to icon-only buttons, and wrote up an honest Accessibility Statement — including what hasn't been tested yet.
