What's Changed

"Built in the open" is easy to put in a tagline. This page is what that actually means: what shipped, what broke and got caught before anyone saw it, and — once they start coming in — what got fixed because someone used the Feedback form.

Three kinds of entries show up here: Shipped for something new, Caught in testing for a bug found and fixed before launch, and Reported for a fix that started with someone else noticing first. That last tag is empty today — it won't stay that way.

Shipped

Canada Paycheck Calculator — all 10 provinces, 3 territories

Fourth country, and the most complex one: federal tax with the Basic Personal Amount credit (including its high-income phase-out), all 13 provincial/territorial bracket tables, Ontario's two-tier surtax, CPP/CPP2, and EI (with Quebec's reduced rate). Quebec uses an approximation for QPP rather than a fully separate model — noted in the disclaimer.

Aug 25, 2026
Caught in testing

Ontario surtax was compounding incorrectly

The two Ontario surtax tiers (20% and 36%) were being applied one on top of the other instead of both independently against the original provincial tax — a subtle bug that only showed up when checked against a known reference rate (20.53% combined marginal at the top bracket; the buggy version gave 21.48%). Fixed before the Canada calculator shipped.

Shipped

Australia Paycheck Calculator

Third country. Federal income tax, the Low Income Tax Offset, and the 2% Medicare levy (with the low-income shade-in) for FY2026-27 — no state income tax to model, since Australia doesn't have one. Checked against an ATO-published worked example ($80,000 income → $16,120 tax and levy) before shipping; matched exactly.

Shipped

Accessibility pass: contrast, keyboard, screen readers

Every text/background color combination on the site had its contrast ratio calculated, not eyeballed — the link color and primary button color were both failing WCAG AA (4.12:1 and 3.45:1, needed 4.5:1) and got darkened just enough to pass. Added skip-to-content links, fixed two skipped heading levels, added labels to icon-only buttons, and wrote up an honest Accessibility Statement — including what hasn't been tested yet.

Caught in testing

ATS checker was dropping real keywords

While building the Career Tools launch, testing turned up two problems before anyone else saw them: short uppercase acronyms like SQL or AWS were getting filtered out if they only appeared once, and trailing punctuation was stopping some real keywords from matching at all. Both fixed before ship.
