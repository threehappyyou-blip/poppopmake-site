        "Portrait" is faster and works best on a person against a fairly simple background. "Complex scene" is slower to load but handles busy backgrounds, multiple subjects, or products better.
        How this works: Merge, split, and image-to-PDF use a JavaScript library (pdf-lib) that runs entirely on your device. Remove Background uses a separate open-source AI model (Transformers.js + MODNet) that downloads once and then also runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server — that also means very large files (100+ MB) may be slow, since your device is doing all the work. There's no file-size cap and no daily limit either way: process one file or a hundred, today or every day. See how to check that yourself, or read our Privacy Policy for more.
