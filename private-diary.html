How your data is protected — exactly

On your device only

Everything you write is stored in your browser on your phone or computer. The app has no server and no account. Its page is set up with a security policy that blocks its scripts from connecting to any other website, and it loads no analytics or ad scripts.

Encrypted before saving

Entries, notes, diary pages and photos are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your passcode (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds) and separately by a recovery code shown to you once.

What it can’t do

We can’t reset your passcode or recover your data. It can’t protect against someone who knows your passcode or malware on your device. A short number passcode is weaker — use a longer one for strong protection.

Questions people ask

Is the diary really private?

Your entries and photos are encrypted and stored only in your browser on your device, behind your passcode. There is no account and no server, so we never receive what you write.

Can I decorate my diary?

Yes. Tap Stickers to add original stickers, washi tape or emoji and drag, turn or resize them; Paper for lines, grid or dots in seven colours; Lettering for handwriting fonts; Draw to doodle; and Page for a week page with a habit tracker, an hourly page or a to-do list. Decorations are encrypted with the entry.

Can I add photos?

Yes, up to 4 per entry. Photos are made smaller (at most 1600 pixels) and encrypted before they are saved on your device.

Are photos included in the backup?

Yes. The backup file contains your diary and photos, still encrypted. Restoring it on another device brings them back.

Can I export my diary?

You can save the diary text as a plain text file from Settings. That file is not encrypted, and photos stay in the app.

What happens if I forget my passcode?

Use the recovery code from setup. If both are lost and no backup opens, nobody can open the diary — not even PopPopMake. Keeping them safe is your responsibility.
