Tab 1

Invoice Generator

Creates a professional invoice as a downloadable PDF, ready to send to a client.

  1. Open the tool. Go to Business Tools and make sure "Invoice Generator" is selected.
  2. Fill in your business details — your name or company name, address, and contact information.
  3. Fill in your client's details the same way.
  4. Add an invoice number and date if not already filled in for you.
  5. Add line items. Click "Add item" for each product or service, filling in a description, quantity, and price. The line total and running total update automatically.
  6. Add tax or a discount if applicable, using the relevant fields.
  7. Add payment terms or notes if you want them on the invoice (like a due date or bank details).
  8. Preview to check everything looks right.
  9. Click "Download PDF."
Good to know: Nothing you enter is saved anywhere between visits — if you send invoices to the same clients regularly, keep a copy of the filled-in details somewhere on your own device so you're not retyping them every time.

Tab 2

QR Code Generator

Turns a link or short text into a scannable QR code, with custom colors, downloadable as a PNG.

  1. Open the tool. Click the "QR Code Generator" tab.
  2. Type or paste your link or text into the input field.
  3. Watch the QR code update live as you type — there's no separate "generate" button to click first.
  4. Customize the colors if you want, using the color pickers for the foreground and background.
  5. Click "Download" to save it as a PNG image.
Good to know: Before printing a QR code anywhere permanent (business cards, signage), scan it yourself with your phone's camera first to confirm it goes to the right place — this is especially important if you customized the colors, since low contrast between foreground and background can make a code harder for some scanners to read.
